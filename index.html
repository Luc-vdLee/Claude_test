How to type figures
  • Leave the figures blank for an unfigured bass: it means a root-position chord, and nothing is printed under the note. Type 5/3 if you want it printed.
  • Stack figures with spaces or /: 6 4, 6/5, 4/3, 4 2. Missing ones are filled in: 6 = 6/3, 7 = 7/5/3.
  • A lone #, b or n (♮) changes the 3rd above the bass.
  • Accidentals on a figure: #6, b7, n6, or after it: 6#, 6+, 6\ (slashed).
  • A sharp raises the note in the key signature by a half step, so in F major # above G turns B♭ into B♮.
  • Use - for figures that follow each other over one bass: 4-3, 9-8, 7-6, 6/4-5/3.

Type a whole bass line
  • Separate notes with spaces. Plain letters follow the key signature; add #, b or n to change that.
  • Octave: C2, G3. Note value: /8, /q, /h (otherwise the selected value).
  • Figures after a colon, with / or , between them: D:6/4/3, E:7,#, G/h:6/4-5/3. Rest: r or r/h.
